# Browser checks on the board session with the 4.0.0 development driver — 2026-10-25

Orange Pi 5B, Plaid Pi image (Firefox 157 via the image's wrapper, Chromium from the image), Wayland session, driver build
77807efc in LIBVA_DRIVERS_PATH, RK_VAAPI_LOG per run; each clip opened as file:// in a fresh profile for 30 s, then the
driver log summarised: VA configs created (profile ids), decode contexts (size, MPP coding id: 7 = H.264, 16777220 = HEVC,
16777224 = AV1), frames MPP returned, exports, refusals/errors, and the High 10 init-QP client decision.
Clips: FREH10-1 (H.264 High 10, 1920x1088, remuxed to mp4), the x265 two-pass HEVC clip (1280x720, 120 frames), Tears of
Steel with libaom film grain (AV1, 1920x1080, 12 frames).

Firefox needs the session environment the image sets (MOZ_DISABLE_RDD_SANDBOX=1, MOZ_X11_EGL=1, MOZ_ENABLE_WAYLAND=1);
launched without it, it loads the driver, probes every profile and decodes everything in software (first block below).
